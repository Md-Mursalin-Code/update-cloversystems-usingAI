Who we are

Eyes in the sky. An edge on the ground.

Clover Systems is a Bangladesh-based drone defence company. We develop unmanned systems around what operators actually need: portability, endurance and reliability.

From long-endurance reconnaissance to rapid-response interception, our fleet is designed to be deployed quickly by small teams in demanding terrain — from coastlines to jungle hills.
